Corruption in the Philippine Government

A major issue affecting poverty and social development

Introduction

Corruption in the Philippine government includes bribery, misuse of public funds, and abuse of power. These actions weaken trust in government and slow national progress.

Forms of Corruption

  • Bribery and illegal payments
  • Overpriced government projects
  • Political favoritism and nepotism
  • Misuse of public funds

Impact on Poverty

Corruption takes money away from education, healthcare, and social services. As a result, many Filipinos—especially children—are forced to live in extreme poverty.

Efforts to Fight Corruption

  • Office of the Ombudsman
  • Commission on Audit (COA)
  • Anti-Red Tape Act
  • Public awareness and media

Conclusion

Ending corruption requires honest leaders and active citizens. Fighting corruption helps reduce poverty and improve the future of the Philippines.
